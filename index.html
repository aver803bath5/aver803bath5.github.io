Multi-Scale Interval Trainer

How to Practice

This trainer helps build visual, muscle, and mental memory for intervals belonging to various musical scales.

Scale Selection:

Use the Practice Scale drop-down menu to load scale formulas including Minor Pentatonic, Major Pentatonic, Natural Major, Natural Minor, Dorian, and Blues scales.

Recommended Routine:
  • Choose your preferred root key.
  • Select your target scale of choice.
  • Use the interval buttons to include/exclude specific steps to narrow down focus.
  • Hit START and match each prompt as quickly as possible on your physical instrument.
